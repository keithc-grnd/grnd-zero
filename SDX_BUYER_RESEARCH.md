# SDX Basin Monument -- Buyer Research

> **GRND-ZERO** | April 2026

---

## Product Summary

The SDX Basin Monument is a physical monitoring device that enables instant visual assessment of sedimentation and debris levels inside retention and detention basins. It uses polished stainless steel rings as visual indicators, is lightweight/modular, and can be installed by a two-person crew without heavy machinery. Born from 30+ years of flood damage observation, it solves a critical gap: poorly maintained basins lose holding volume to sediment buildup, leading to overflow and flooding.

---

## Why These Companies Will Want the SDX

The U.S. stormwater management market is projected to grow from **$18.8B (2025) to $41.9B by 2035** (CAGR 8.36%). Key trends driving urgency:

- **41% of cities** are adopting smart stormwater monitoring systems
- IoT-based flow monitoring usage **increased 46% in 2024 alone**
- Regulatory pressure from MS4/NPDES permits demands better inspection and compliance tooling
- Climate-driven flooding is intensifying, making basin performance life-or-death infrastructure

The SDX fills a gap that no one else occupies: **simple, low-cost, zero-power visual sedimentation monitoring** that complements both legacy infrastructure and smart IoT platforms.

## TIER 1 -- Strategic Acquirers (Would Buy the Company/IP)

These are large companies actively acquiring stormwater technology. The SDX slots directly into their product portfolios.

### 1. Advanced Drainage Systems (ADS)
- **Revenue:** ~$2.9B (FY2026)
- **Why they're hungry:** ADS just closed a **$1 billion acquisition of NDS** (Feb 2026) to expand stormwater and irrigation. Also acquired River Valley Pipe and Orenco Systems in the last 18 months. They are on a tear consolidating the entire stormwater value chain.
- **Fit:** The SDX would give ADS a monitoring/maintenance product to sell alongside their drainage pipes, chambers, and treatment systems. They currently have zero basin-condition monitoring products.
- **Contact path:** Investor relations / BD at ads-pipe.com

### 2. Contech Engineered Solutions
- **Parent:** Quikrete Holdings (acquired via Forterra)
- **Why they're hungry:** Contech acquired Bio Clean and Kahn Culvert. They sell detention/retention systems (StormGenie, ChamberMaxx, UrbanPond) but have **no monitoring or inspection tool** in their portfolio.
- **Fit:** Perfect product-line extension. Every basin they sell needs monitoring -- the SDX makes their value prop stickier.
- **Contact path:** conteches.com / BD team

### 3. Oldcastle Infrastructure
- **Parent:** CRH plc (~$35B revenue)
- **Why they're hungry:** Already partnered with OptiRTC for their "StormCapture CMAC" smart module. They run a **Certified Maintenance Provider network** for installed stormwater systems. The SDX is a natural add to their maintenance ecosystem.
- **Fit:** Physical monitoring device that their maintenance provider network can install and inspect. Low-tech complement to their smart platform.
- **Contact path:** oldcastleinfrastructure.com

### 4. StormTrap
- **Why they're hungry:** Acquired Faircloth Skimmer (July 2025). Building a full-suite stormwater solutions company (SingleTrap, DoubleTrap, SiteSaver, TrashTrap, PumpGuard). Missing monitoring/inspection products.
- **Fit:** Add SDX to every StormTrap installation as an upsell. Creates recurring inspection/replacement revenue.
- **Contact path:** stormtrap.com

## TIER 2 -- Smart Water / IoT Platform Companies

These companies sell digital monitoring and would want the SDX as a physical hardware complement or data-collection entry point.

### 5. Xylem Inc.
- **Revenue:** ~$8B+ (post-Evoqua)
- **Why they're hungry:** Acquired Evoqua ($7.5B), Idrica (digital water platform), Valor Water Analytics, Sensus ($1.7B). Invested **$20M in VC funds** for early-stage water technology. They have an explicit innovation mandate.
- **Fit:** The SDX could be the low-cost physical entry point that feeds data into Xylem Vue (their digital platform). Add a sensor to the SDX rings and it becomes a connected device.
- **Contact path:** xylem.com/innovation or Xylem Watermark venture program

### 6. OptiRTC (Opti)
- **Why they're hungry:** Leading provider of Continuous Monitoring and Adaptive Control (CMAC) for stormwater. Cloud-based platform that integrates third-party sensors. They need more physical-world data sources.
- **Fit:** The SDX would give Opti a branded hardware product. Currently they're software-only and depend on partner hardware.
- **Contact path:** optirtc.com

### 7. Hydro International
- **Why they're hungry:** Global stormwater treatment and control company with strong R&D focus. They sell Downstream Defender, First Defense -- all treatment, no monitoring.
- **Fit:** The SDX gives them a monitoring product to cross-sell with treatment systems.
- **Contact path:** hydro-int.com
