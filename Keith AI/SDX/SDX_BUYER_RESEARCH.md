## What SDX 2026 Is

SDX 2026 is a browser-based storm drain hydraulic analysis platform built over 30 years of professional flood control practice. It is the only commercially available tool that integrates native HEC-22 inlet analysis, full HGL/EGL hydraulic grade line analysis, NOAA Atlas 14 precipitation, Modified Puls basin routing, pre-built U.S. agency compliance libraries, and direct Civil 3D data extraction into a single production-ready environment. It performs the same analyses as InfoDrainage at 10-15x the speed with output U.S. agencies accept directly. Asking price: $40M.

---

## Why NOW -- The Autodesk Validation

**Critical intelligence:** Autodesk just shipped a "Drainage Analysis Technical Preview" in Civil 3D 2026. This is cloud-based, depends on InfoDrainage services, and is early-stage. This means:

1. Autodesk is **admitting InfoDrainage doesn't work** for the U.S. market
2. They're **trying to build internally** what SDX already does
3. Their preview is incomplete -- no pre-built agency compliance, no standalone speed advantage
4. **The window is closing.** In 2-3 years, Autodesk may get "good enough." Any competitor who acquires SDX now has a 3-year head start.

Every company below should hear this: **Autodesk is building this. If you don't acquire it, they will eventually have it -- and you won't.**
