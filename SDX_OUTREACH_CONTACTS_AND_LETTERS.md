## LETTER 1: TO ADVANCED DRAINAGE SYSTEMS

**To:** Michael Higgins, VP Corporate Strategy & Investor Relations
**From:** Keith Christiansen, Founder, GRND-ZERO
**Subject:** Basin monitoring product that completes your stormwater portfolio

---

Mr. Higgins,

I'm writing because Advanced Drainage Systems has built the most complete stormwater product line in North America -- pipes, chambers, treatment, infiltration -- but there is one gap: after a basin is installed, there is no ADS product that tells the owner whether it's still working.

Sedimentation silently destroys basin capacity. A retention basin rated for 100,000 gallons that has lost 30% of its volume to sediment buildup is a flooding liability, not an asset. Municipalities and engineers have no fast, low-cost way to assess this in the field.

That's the problem GRND-ZERO's SDX Basin Monument solves.

The SDX is a physical monitoring device that installs inside retention and detention basins. It uses polished stainless steel visual indicators that allow any field crew to instantly assess sedimentation and debris levels -- no sensors, no power, no subscriptions. A two-person crew installs it in under an hour.

**Why this matters to ADS specifically:**

- **Product-line completion.** Every StormTech chamber, every HP pipe system, every Nyloplast basin you sell should ship with a monitoring solution. The SDX is that solution.
- **Recurring revenue.** Inspection creates maintenance cycles. Maintenance creates replacement and service revenue. The SDX makes ADS relevant after the sale, not just at installation.
- **Regulatory tailwind.** MS4/NPDES compliance is forcing municipalities to prove they inspect basins. The SDX is the fastest, cheapest way to demonstrate compliance.
- **Acquisition fit.** Your recent acquisitions of NDS ($1B), River Valley Pipe, and Orenco show you're building a full-lifecycle water management company. The SDX adds the monitoring layer you don't have yet.

I've been studying sedimentation and flood damage for over 30 years. The SDX was built from that fieldwork. I'd welcome 20 minutes to walk you through the product and discuss how it fits into ADS's portfolio strategy.

I can send a physical unit to your Hilliard office for your team to evaluate.

Best regards,

Keith Christiansen

**To:** Houston Spencer, Corporate Communications / Snehal Desai, EVP Chief Growth & Innovation Officer
**From:** Keith Christiansen, Founder, GRND-ZERO
**Subject:** Physical basin monitoring device for your water technology platform

---

Mr. Spencer / Mr. Desai,

Xylem has committed $50 million to corporate venture investments in water technologies that address scarcity, quality, and infrastructure resilience. I believe GRND-ZERO's SDX Basin Monument is exactly the kind of technology that commitment was designed to find.

The SDX is a physical monitoring device installed inside retention and detention basins. It provides instant visual assessment of sedimentation and debris levels using polished stainless steel indicators -- no power, no connectivity required. A two-person crew installs it in under an hour.

There are over 1 million retention and detention basins across the United States. The vast majority have no monitoring of any kind. Sedimentation silently destroys their holding capacity, turning flood-prevention infrastructure into flood-causing infrastructure. The SDX solves this at a fraction of the cost of IoT sensor systems.

**Why this matters to Xylem specifically:**

- **Hardware entry point for Xylem Vue.** The SDX is currently a standalone visual device. Add a connected sensor to the stainless steel indicators and it becomes a low-cost IoT node feeding real-time sedimentation data into Xylem's digital water platform. This is the physical-to-digital bridge Xylem needs for basin monitoring.
- **Complements your portfolio.** Xylem sells pumps, treatment, analytics, and sensors -- but nothing purpose-built for basin sedimentation monitoring. The SDX fills that gap.

This product was built from 30+ years of field observation of flood damage caused by unmaintained basins. I'd welcome the opportunity to discuss how the SDX fits into Xylem's growth and innovation strategy, whether as a venture investment, a licensing deal, or an acquisition.
